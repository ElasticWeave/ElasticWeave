# Scorecard

Score each dimension from 1 to 10.

## Reader fit

10 means the draft clearly serves one reader and one decision.

Low-score signs:

- too many audiences;
- generic “stakeholders”;
- no next step;
- background before answer.

## First-screen clarity

10 means the reader understands the point without scrolling.

Low-score signs:

- intro starts with market context;
- no concrete proof;
- no quick start for README;
- conclusion buried.

## Direct fact yield

10 means representative passages deliver many source-supported, independently checkable facts per
reading effort. Use `fact-density-and-metaphor.md` to sample and normalize the comparison.

Low-score signs:

- robust, seamless, powerful;
- repeated conclusions counted as new information;
- numbers without subject, unit, baseline, or condition;
- citations that do not say what they support;
- vague “users benefit.”

## Literal clarity and metaphor cost

10 means the reader reaches the claim without translating decorative metaphors or switching between
inconsistent analogy systems. Established technical terms are defined and used consistently.

Low-score signs:

- results “survive,” evidence “fights,” and costs “eat” gains when literal wording is shorter;
- headings advertise drama rather than state results;
- a metaphor hides the actor, measurement, condition, or uncertainty;
- several metaphor families describe the same mechanism.

## Readability under compression

10 means the text is compact but still easy for the intended reader to parse on the first pass.

Low-score signs:

- stacked nouns or undefined acronyms;
- qualifiers whose target is ambiguous;
- dense tables or visual labels that require zooming;
- caveats removed to improve the ratio;
- triads everywhere;
- em-dash padding;
- bold-first bullets;
- passive voice that hides the actor.

## Source fidelity

10 means facts, caveats, and citations survived.

Low-score signs:

- invented claims;
- removed caveats;
- changed code semantics;
- unsupported confidence.

## Thresholds

- 54–60: ship.
- 45–53: light edit.
- 35–44: restructure one or more sections.
- Below 35: rewrite from the reader's task.
