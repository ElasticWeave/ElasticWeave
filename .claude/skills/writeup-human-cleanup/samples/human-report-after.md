# Expansion depends on onboarding, not pricing

Customers did not cite price as the main blocker. They cited setup time, unclear handoffs, and slow first-value moments. Fund onboarding improvements before changing the pricing page.

## Evidence

- 42% of stalled opportunities mentioned setup time.
- Pricing appeared in 9% of loss notes.
- Accounts that completed onboarding within 14 days expanded 2.1x more often.

## Caveat

The sample covers enterprise deals only. SMB behavior may differ.
